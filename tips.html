A FEW NOTES FOR THE ROAD

Travel Notes

Your suitcase deserves breathing room

If you plan to bring home clothes, souvenirs, or a suspicious number of snacks, do not pack your suitcase to its limit! Leave room for your finds. Your future self should not have to sit on the suitcase to close it. Check your airline's baggage limits before deciding that another bag is the answer.

Cute shoes? Yes. Blisters? No.

Bring comfortable shoes that you have already broken in. A sightseeing day can turn into a walking marathon, and your feet did not sign up to suffer for the outfit. Pack for the weather and give yourself permission to take a cafe break.

Make plans, leave room for plot twists

Your itinerary is a guide, not your boss. Pick a few must-see places, but leave room to wander. Do not let TikTok plan every meal! Step away from the famous spots and explore quieter streets or less popular places to dine. There is so much amazing food to discover beyond the viral recommendations. A little restaurant you stumble upon might become your favorite memory. Check opening hours, stay curious, and give yourself time to enjoy the unexpected.

Low battery is not a personality trait

Charge your phone before heading out and consider bringing a portable charger. Save your accommodation details and important directions offline. Being lost is less charming when your phone is at one percent.

Be curious, be kind

Follow posted rules, keep shared spaces tidy, and be considerate of the people around you. Ask before taking someone's photo, and check food ingredients directly with restaurants if you have dietary needs. Good manners belong in every carry-on.

Tap, ride, snack, repeat

A train ride and a convenience store snack run? Suica can help with both! If you have a compatible iPhone, the Welcome Suica Mobile app lets you set up and top up a digital card. Use it on participating trains and buses, and at convenience stores that accept Suica. Look for the Suica or compatible IC logo, and check your balance before heading out. Your snack budget might disappear faster than you think!

The Welcome Suica Mobile app is for compatible iPhones. If your phone is not supported, check physical Welcome Suica card options instead.
